Core expertise

From requirements to useful evidence

My work spans communication systems, simulation and data analysis. Across these settings, I define measurable questions, build reproducible workflows and compare outcomes under realistic conditions.

01 / MODEL

Model systems and scenarios

Translate requirements into models of topology, radio conditions, traffic and mobility. At Fraunhofer IIS, I combined network and vehicle simulation to study 5G/6G performance.

OMNeT++ · Simu5G · INET · Veins · SUMO
02 / DECIDE

Design and compare solutions

Develop reference methods and compare resource-allocation strategies, including reinforcement learning, against defined QoS and system constraints.

NR sidelink · resource allocation · reinforcement learning
03 / EVALUATE

Analyse and communicate results

Use Python, statistical analysis and visualisation to examine reliability, latency, throughput and energy trade-offs. Build interactive applications when exploration helps others understand the results.

KPI design · simulation campaigns · statistical analysis

FRAUNHOFER IIS · 2021–2025

Autonomous NR V2X resource selection

Studied distributed resource allocation for NR sidelink Mode 2, including energy-aware selection and QoS-driven single-agent and multi-agent learning. Evaluated latency, reliability and resource use across traffic and channel conditions; results contributed to peer-reviewed IEEE papers.

Combined Swiss population, income and OpenStreetMap retail data to compare candidate store locations. Cleaned postal-code data, assessed nearby competitors and catchment areas, and made the analysis explorable in Streamlit.
